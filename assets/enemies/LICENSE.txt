LPC zombies in this folder

zombie_stand.png and zombie_hold.png stack two layers from the Universal LPC
Spritesheet Character Generator:

https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator

- body/bodies/zombie/walk/zombie.png
- head/heads/zombie/adult/walk.png

Both layers list CC-BY-SA 3.0 and GPL 3.0 in CREDITS.csv. This game uses
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC-BY-SA 3.0):

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode

Authors, from CREDITS.csv: Stephen Challener (Redshrike), Johannes Sjölund
(wulax), Sander Frenken (castelonia), Benjamin K. Smith (BenCreating), and
bluecarrot16.

Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-zombie

zombie_stand.png is walk column 1. zombie_hold.png is walk column 4. Both are
the front row, scaled 2x with nearest-neighbor filtering.
