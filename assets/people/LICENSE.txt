LPC villagers in this folder

These PNGs are composites of Liberated Pixel Cup layers from the Universal LPC
Spritesheet Character Generator:

https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator

The combined images are shared under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
3.0 Unported (CC-BY-SA 3.0):

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode

Where a source file offered more than one license, CC-BY-SA 3.0 is the one
used here. The short-sleeve t-shirt is OGA-BY 3.0 only. OGA-BY 3.0 section
8(g) says: "You may distribute this work under the terms of the Creative
Commons Attribution 3.0 license (CC-BY 3.0)." The t-shirt is stacked with
CC-BY-SA layers, so the finished villager PNGs are under CC-BY-SA 3.0.

man_blue.png shifts the shirt hue to blue. man_old.png shifts the shirt hue
toward olive and turns the messy1 hair gray. Those two edits were made for
this game.

Each PNG is the full walk cycle, 9 columns by 4 rows, scaled 2x with
nearest-neighbor filtering. Row 0 is up (back), row 1 is left, row 2 is down
(face toward the camera), and row 3 is right.

Credit the authors named in CREDITS.md. The layer list, authors, and source
pages are copied from that repository's CREDITS.csv.
